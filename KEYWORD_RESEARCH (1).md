## 3. DOMINANT SEARCH INTENT AND AUDIENCE EXPECTATIONS

**Dominant intent [INFERRED]: local, urgent-but-not-emergency, appointment intent.**
The visitor has a sick child now. They're comparing "can I get in at my pediatrician / a pediatric office today" versus urgent care, retail clinic or ER. Time matters more than detail.

**What they expect immediately after the click [INFERRED]:**
1. Confirmation this is a pediatric office near them (location name in hero).
2. Whether they can be seen today and how (sick call hours, no appointment, or call to book).
3. A phone number they can tap.
4. Whether testing happens on-site.
5. A clear line on when to go to the ER instead.

**Secondary intent [INFERRED]: diagnostic/testing.** Parent suspects flu/RSV/strep (often from a daycare or school notice) and wants confirmation. Overlaps heavily with the dominant intent — same page works.

**Informational intent (not appointment-ready):** symptom questions such as "RSV symptoms in babies" or "how long is flu contagious." These visitors want to learn, not book. They are low-intent for a call-focused page and costly as paid clicks; see Sections 4 and 7.
