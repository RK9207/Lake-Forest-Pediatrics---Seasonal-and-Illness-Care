What a sick visit can help clarify

Colds, flu, RSV, and other illnesses can share symptoms such as cough, fever, congestion, and sore throat. A pediatric sick visit gives your child a clinical evaluation and gives you a chance to ask what might be causing the symptoms, how to keep your child comfortable, and what to watch for at home.

On-site testing can help the clinician make a care recommendation when symptoms alone leave questions. Your child’s clinician will decide whether testing is useful and whether any follow-up is needed.

On-site testing when needed

Flu
A rapid test can help the clinician check for influenza and discuss care options.
RSV
Testing can help identify RSV when it’s hard to tell apart from other respiratory illnesses.
Strep
A rapid test can help determine whether strep is causing a sore throat. A throat culture may be needed for follow-up.

Questions about sick visits

Do I need an appointment?

Morning Sick Call runs from 8–9 a.m. on participating days and does not require an appointment. For care outside that window, call (847) 295-1220 to ask about a sick appointment. Same-day visits depend on availability.

How does Morning Sick Call check-in work?

During the 8–9 a.m. window, go to the office parking lot and register through the practice website. Check the schedule for your office before leaving home. If you’re new to the practice or unsure about check-in, call first.

Is Morning Sick Call available on Saturdays?

Lake Bluff and Lindenhurst offer Saturday Morning Sick Call from 8–9 a.m. Vernon Hills offers Morning Sick Call Monday–Friday.

What if my child gets sick at night?

Call (847) 295-1220 for guidance. Medical professionals are on call 24/7 by phone. For an emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department.

How long do flu, RSV, or strep results take?

Timing depends on the test. Some tests give rapid results, and samples sent to a lab can take longer. Ask the care team what timing to expect and how you’ll receive results.

Can my child be seen if we’re new to the practice?

Call the office before coming in to ask about eligibility for Morning Sick Call or a scheduled sick visit.

How do I check if you accept my insurance?

Call the practice with the name of your insurance plan to confirm participation and ask what to bring. Check with your insurer if you have questions about your individual coverage.
