\#\# IMG-HERO-01 \- Hero

\#\#\# Placeholder purpose

Help a caregiver recognize a familiar sick-day moment and feel ready to take the next step. The page copy communicates local pediatric sick visits, testing, and the call option. The image supplies context and reassurance without suggesting a specific diagnosis, availability, or recovery.

\#\#\# Recommended source type

AI-generated lifestyle/editorial image appropriate

\#\#\# Recommended composition

Use a 4:3 landscape master for the artwork on the right of the desktop copy. Keep both faces and any meaningful gesture within the center 70% of the frame, slightly right of center and above the lower third. Keep the bottom edge, especially the lower left, low-detail for the existing 24/7 phone-support card. On mobile, the artwork follows the headline, testing copy, and CTAs, and crops to 3:2. Check that the shorter crop and the card preserve both faces and the key gesture. Widescreen empty space for a headline is unnecessary because the copy sits outside the image.

\#\#\# Option 1 \- A quiet moment on the couch

Create a photorealistic 4:3 landscape editorial photograph for a pediatric sick-visit page. On a cool Illinois morning, a caregiver in their 30s sits close to a six- to eight-year-old child on a linen sofa at home. The child, in a soft blue long-sleeve shirt, looks a little tired and rests against the caregiver, who turns toward them with a calm, attentive expression. Neither looks at the camera. Use a natural seated eye-level composition, 50 mm lens feel, soft window light, realistic skin texture, and a lived-in but quiet room with warm neutrals and muted sage. Place both faces in the upper central area, slightly right of center, with room above their heads. Let the lower third fall mostly on a plain throw and sofa fabric, with especially low detail at the lower left for the page overlay. Preserve the expressions in a centered 3:2 crop. No lettering, badges, logos, medical props, staged smiles, or exaggerated illness.

\#\#\# Option 2 \- Making the call from home

Create a photorealistic 4:3 landscape documentary-style still for a local pediatric sick-care landing page. A caregiver in their 30s sits beside a four- to six-year-old at a kitchen table, holding a phone naturally to their ear while listening. The child leans against the caregiver’s shoulder in a muted blue sweatshirt. Capture the shared posture and the caregiver’s focused expression from across the table at seated eye level, with a 35 mm lens feel, gently diffused morning daylight, soft cream and sage tones, and enough depth of field to keep both faces readable. Place the caregiver, child, and phone in the upper middle of the frame, slightly right of center. Keep the foreground table and lower-left area quiet for a separate page overlay, with both faces and the hand holding the phone clear in a 3:2 crop. Keep the screen out of view, preserve natural finger anatomy, and omit text, logos, medical props, and posed expressions.

\#\#\# Option 3 \- Getting ready to head out

Create a realistic 4:3 landscape environmental lifestyle photograph for pediatric seasonal sick care. In an ordinary northern Illinois home entryway on an overcast fall morning, a caregiver in their late 30s crouches and helps a seven- to nine-year-old into a light sage jacket. The child stands close, slightly subdued but comfortable. Frame from the child’s eye level with a 40 mm lens feel. Use soft light from a frosted door window, warm neutral walls, and a suggestion of muted autumn foliage outside. Put the faces and jacket gesture above mid-frame, with breathing room for the 3:2 mobile crop. Keep the bottom edge and lower-left corner softly textured and clear for the page overlay. Show a familiar family routine without staged smiles, a facility entrance, visible street identifiers, text, branding, or signs of acute distress.

\#\#\# Recommended option

\*\*Option 1 \- A quiet moment on the couch.\*\* It captures the parent’s concern through proximity and expression, and the foreground fabric gives the phone-support card room without hiding the subjects. It applies equally well to all three offices.

\#\# IMG-PROVIDER-01 \- Pediatric team

\#\#\# Placeholder purpose

Put a human face on the practice’s pediatric expertise before the parent reviews. The image sits beside the “A pediatric team close to home” copy and should help families recognize the team and feel comfortable seeking care.

\#\#\# Recommended source type

Real provider/team image preferred

\#\#\# Recommended composition

Use a 4:5 vertical portrait, with eyes in the upper third and face and shoulders near the center. Leave modest space around the head and enough room at the right edge for a subject whose body turns slightly toward the adjacent desktop copy. There is no text overlay. The slot displays up to 300 px wide on desktop and becomes a narrower portrait below the text on small screens. Favor one face or a tight pair whose faces remain legible at that size. Keep background detail low and preview the actual mobile crop.

\#\#\# Option 1 \- Approachable environmental portrait

Generate a photorealistic 4:5 vertical editorial reference for a commissioned pediatric clinician portrait. Show a fictional clinician in their 40s in a generic community pediatric office, framed from the chest up and looking toward the camera with a relaxed, understated smile. The clinician stands with shoulders angled gently toward frame right in a neat muted-navy shirt and simple unbranded layer. Place the eyes near the upper third and give the head and shoulders room on all sides. Use soft window light, an 85 mm portrait lens feel, natural skin detail, and shallow separation from a warm light-gray background. Preserve the face in a compact mobile presentation. No badges, readable text, prominent equipment, white-coat theatrics, or resemblance to an identifiable real provider.

\#\#\# Option 2 \- Listening at the child’s level

Generate a photorealistic 4:5 vertical documentary reference for a possible commissioned pediatric care photograph. Show a fictional clinician in their late 30s seated at eye level with a school-aged child, whose shoulder is softly out of focus in the foreground. The clinician listens with a thoughtful expression and relaxed posture, dressed in muted blue professional clothing. Use a low-contrast cream and sage room, diffused side light, and a 50 mm lens feel. Frame the clinician’s face slightly right of center in the upper half, leaving room around it for mobile and keeping the foreground at the outer edge. Use believable skin detail and eye lines. Omit testing equipment, identifiable paperwork, labels, logos, dramatic expressions, and any resemblance to the actual clinic or its staff.

\#\#\# Option 3 \- A small-team portrait between visits

Generate a photorealistic 4:5 vertical editorial reference for a commissioned image of two pediatric team members. Show two fictional clinicians in their 30s to 50s conversing beside a softly lit interior window in a generic community practice. One faces slightly toward the camera while the other responds with a subtle, candid expression. Use understated professional clothing in slate and muted green, simple pale wood and neutral wall finishes, and a 50 mm lens feel at eye level. Frame both people chest-up, with faces close and near the same focal plane in the upper middle so they remain readable in a small 4:5 crop. Leave modest edge space, keep the background quiet, and avoid posed group-photo smiles, stethoscope props, logos, signage, name badges, and likenesses of real staff.

\#\#\# Recommended option

\*\*Option 1 \- Approachable environmental portrait\*\*, as the brief for an approved real photo. A single recognizable face stays clear at the slot’s mobile size and pairs naturally with the “Meet the pediatric team” link.
